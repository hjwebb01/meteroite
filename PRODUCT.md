# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers reviewing pull requests, including large ones handed to them by others, who need to know where to look first.

## Product Purpose

Meteroite reviews GitHub pull requests. Paste a pull request URL and it reports findings, lets the reviewer discuss or investigate each one against the pinned source, proposes fixes, and can publish the completed review back to GitHub.

## Positioning

Read by purpose: the pull request is split into purposes, each is read and marked read, and a brief says where to start. (Confirmed as the claim a neighboring review tool could not copy. The README's "findings ranked by confidence" is the older framing.)

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Terms: Review, Finding, Finding work, Proposal, Application, Check (see GLOSSARY.md).
- Sign-in access and permissions shown to visitors are undecided; do not state them on the landing page.
- Brand spelling "meteroite" is intentional.

## Brand Commitments

- The name and spelling "meteroite".
- The meteorite metaphor already on the landing (etching an iron meteorite compared to splitting a PR by purpose). Whether the credited photographs stay is an open decision, not a commitment.

## Evidence on Hand

- Sample review and publication demo fixtures (illustrative, not live model output) in `src/features/auth/components/`.
- Four credited meteorite photographs in `public/meteorites`.
- No customers, testimonials, benchmarks or pricing exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Be honest about what is sample and what is real; label illustrative output.
- Show the real product, not descriptions of it.
- Nothing publishes to GitHub unseen.
- Lead with where to start reading, not with how much was found.
